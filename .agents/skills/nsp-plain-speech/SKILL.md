# Plain speech

## Human surfaces
Apply to assistant-authored replies and prose a human will read. Keep bot-only state, receipts, inventories, and machine handoffs as compact fields and references; they do not need a prose pass.

## Pass
Lead with the outcome. Use concrete subjects, active verbs, familiar words, and actual project terms. Explain evidence and limits where they affect a decision. Remove filler, canned transitions, inflated claims, vague attribution, redundant summaries, and decorative headings. Use lists for parallel or sequential information. State the intended action without inventing a worse alternative to contrast with it.

## Boundaries
Preserve facts, uncertainty, safety and legal language, quotations, code, schemas, source material, and requested tone. This pass does not authorize edits to those surfaces or replace validation, routing, or evidence. It needs no artifact or execution workflow.
